ABOUT 2BITARCADE

🔓 FREE. FOR EVERYONE. NO CATCH.

Every cabinet in the arcade is free to play, for anyone, with no wallet, no token, and no trial clock. Insert coin, or don't — either way, you play.

FULLY OPEN SOURCE

The entire 2bitArcade — every game, the site itself, and the backend functions — is MIT-licensed and hosted on GitHub Pages. Read it, fork it, run your own copy, build on it. See the LICENSE file in the repo for the exact terms (third-party games keep their own original licenses, listed in LICENSES.md).

WHY THE CHANGE

2bitArcade launched with a token-gated access model. We've since moved to a fully free, fully open model instead — simpler for players, and truer to what we actually wanted to build: a good arcade, not a paywall.

BUILD WITH US

Are you a game developer? The 2bitArcade is an open cabinet floor. Build a browser game and we'll host it here beside JumpFighter and the rest. Hit CONTACT US at the top of the page and pitch your game.

ROADMAP

More games, continuously. The cabinet floor keeps growing — our own titles plus properly-licensed games from other builders.

LEADERBOARDS

Every day, the top 3 players across the arcade get featured on the champions board. Every week, the top 10 get the same. No unlocks attached — just bragging rights.
